Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

🔒 AIChatSweep does not collect, store, or transmit any user data. All operations run entirely in your browser.

Overview

AIChatSweep is a browser extension that helps you manage conversations across AI chat platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). It provides bulk selection, deletion, and archiving of conversations directly in your browser.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed with a zero data collection principle — we never access, read, or transmit the content of your conversations.

What We Do NOT Collect

Data Type Collected?
Conversation content No
Personal information (name, email, etc.) No
Browsing history No
Authentication tokens or passwords No
Usage analytics or telemetry No
IP address or geolocation No

How the Extension Works

1. Content Scripts (Checkbox Injection)

The extension injects visual checkboxes into the sidebar of supported AI platforms. This only modifies the appearance of the page in your browser — no data is read or sent anywhere.

2. Conversation Management

When you delete or archive conversations, the extension calls the platform's own API directly from your browser using your existing login session. We act as a shortcut — the same as if you manually deleted each conversation one by one.

3. No Data Storage

The extension does not store any data — no preferences, no conversation data, nothing. Your selections exist only in the page while it is open and are gone when you close it.

Permissions Explained

Permission Why We Need It
host_permissions To inject checkboxes on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini pages. These permissions allow content scripts to run on those specific sites only. No other permissions are requested.

Third-Party Services

AIChatSweep does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or data processing services.

The extension communicates only with the AI platforms you are already logged into (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini), using their existing APIs and your existing browser session.

No External Servers

AIChatSweep does not operate any backend servers. There is no server to send data to. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Since we do not collect any user data, material changes are unlikely.
