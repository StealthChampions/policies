Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

🔒 AIChatSweep sends no data to the developer or any third party — no telemetry, no analytics, no backend of its own. The only thing it stores is your light/dark theme preference, kept in the extension's own local storage.

Overview

AIChatSweep is a browser extension that helps you manage conversations across AI chat platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). It provides bulk selection, deletion, and archiving of conversations directly in your browser.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This extension is designed with a zero data collection principle — we never access, read, or transmit the content of your conversations.

What We Do NOT Collect

Data Type Collected?
Conversation content No
Personal information (name, email, etc.) No
Browsing history No
Authentication tokens or passwords No
Usage analytics or telemetry No
IP address or geolocation No

"Collected" means sent to us or to a third party. Nothing in this table is ever transmitted off your device. Where the extension needs your existing session to act on your behalf, it uses it only within the site you are already on — see below.

How the Extension Works

1. Content Scripts (Checkbox Injection)

The extension injects visual checkboxes into the sidebar of supported AI platforms. This only modifies the appearance of the page in your browser — no data is read or sent anywhere.

2. Conversation Management

When you delete or archive conversations, the extension acts as a shortcut for the clicks you would otherwise make yourself. Every request it issues goes to the same site you are already on (same-origin, relative URLs), reusing your existing logged-in session:

  • ChatGPT — calls the site's own endpoints: /api/auth/session to read your session token, DELETE/PATCH on /backend-api/conversation/:id to delete or archive, and /backend-api/conversations to recover from an expired token.
  • Claude — calls the site's own endpoints: /api/organizations to read your organization id, then DELETE /api/organizations/:orgId/chat_conversations/:id.
  • Gemini — makes no network requests at all. The extension drives Gemini's own on-page UI (open the row's ⋮ menu → Delete → confirm), so every resulting request is issued by the Gemini page itself, exactly as if you had clicked by hand.

Your session token is read from the site's own endpoint and used only in requests back to that same site. It is never stored, logged, or sent anywhere else.

What IS Stored (Locally)

Theme Preference — and Nothing Else

The extension stores exactly one value: your theme choice (auto / light / dark), written under the key acs-theme in the extension popup's own localStorage. It never leaves your browser. chrome.storage is deliberately not used, which is why the extension needs no storage permission.

Nothing Else Is Persisted

No conversation content, message text, account information, or other personal data is ever saved. Your checkbox selections live in memory only and are gone the moment the page closes.

Permissions Explained

Permission Why We Need It
host_permissions To run content scripts on the four supported domains only — chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com, claude.ai, and gemini.google.com. This is what lets the extension inject checkboxes and act on those pages.
Chrome API permissions None. The extension requests no storage, no tabs, no broad host access, and no remote code.

Third-Party Services

AIChatSweep does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, advertising, or data processing services. It contains no remote code, no eval, no third-party SDK, and no in-product reporting or feedback channel.

The extension communicates only with the AI platforms you are already logged into (ChatGPT and Claude), using their existing APIs and your existing browser session. On Gemini it issues no requests at all.

No External Servers

AIChatSweep does not operate any backend servers, and has no update server of its own. Every request URL the extension issues is same-origin — no data is transmitted to any external or developer-controlled server. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Children's Privacy

This extension is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.
